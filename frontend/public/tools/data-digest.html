Choose or drop a CSV, TSV, JSON or JSONL file, paste text on the left, or press Load sample.

You get one card per column (type, nulls, distinct values, ranges, top values) and a digest on the right that is usually tens to thousands of times smaller than the file.

The digest appears here once data is loaded. The Prompt tab is plain text you can paste straight into an LLM chat.
