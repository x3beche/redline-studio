MUST
Printed-to-printed sliding fits get 0.25 mm clearance per side; printed-to-bought-part fits get 0.20 mm per side.FDM holes print undersized and printed pins print oversized, so tolerances must be set per fit class.
MUST
Add 0.3 mm to the nominal diameter of printed holes for bought hardware clearance, then 0.4 mm for a normal clearance fit (clear_hole = d + 0.4 + 0.3).Printed holes close up, so a nominal hole grips the screw or insert too tightly.
MUST
Self-tap screw pilot holes in printed plastic get no added clearance (M2 1.7 mm, M3 2.5 mm).The screw compresses the plastic to hold; extra clearance lets it spin loose.
MUST
Minimum wall thickness is 0.8 mm for new features on a 0.4 mm nozzle (two perimeters); anything thinner is redesigned, not printed.A 0.2 mm wall cannot be printed reliably and was rejected in review.
MUST
Every printed overhang is at least 45 degrees from horizontal; angles are computed and checked in the build, which fails if violated.

MUST
Fan connector is 4-pin (1 GND, 2 +12 V, 3 RPM tach, 4 PWM) so the fan speed is set by PWM and the tach is read back.Switching the fan's ground to control speed breaks RPM readback on a 4-wire fan.
PREFER
Connectors and headers that must exit the back edge are right-angle (90 degree) through-hole parts with pins facing outward from the board edge.Lets the mating cable or jumper come out of the case without bending.
PREFER
Display header replaced by bare 2.54 mm pad holes (1.0 mm drill, 2.0 mm pad) so a module can sit flush against the case.A plug-in header adds height and blocks a flush mount.
PREFER
Choose parts from LCSC in stock, preferring JLCPCB Basic parts, and record the LCSC number on each component.Keeps the BOM orderable and assembly cost low.
MUST
Board DRC must show zero courtyard overlaps and zero unconnected pads, and ERC must show zero errors before the design is released.Courtyard overlaps and unconnected pads are real build errors.
